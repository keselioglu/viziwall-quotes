Viziwall – LED Screen General Rental Terms & Conditions

1. Scope of Services

Viziwall provides supply, delivery, installation, commissioning, dismantling, and removal of LED screen systems, including technical support, as defined in the accepted quotation.

Audio systems and dynamic content changes are not included unless explicitly agreed in advance.

2. Customer Responsibilities

  • In hanging led wall rentals the costs related to hanging services provided by the exhibition organizer are to be covered by the client.
  • The Customer is responsible for ensuring the installation site is suitable, safe, accessible, level, and equipped with sufficient power.
  • All images and video content must be delivered in correct format and resolution at least 7 days before the event.
  • The Customer must ensure representatives are present during installation and commissioning.
  • The Customer must provide entrance ticket for the event days for 2 technicians of Viziwall.

3. Changes & Additional Services

Any changes or additional services must be requested in writing and are subject to Viziwall’s approval and additional charges.

4. Prices & Payment Terms

  • All prices and payment terms are based on the accepted quotation.
  • Payments must be made by bank transfer only.
  • Late payments incur 1.5% monthly interest.

5. Installation Site Requirements

The installation site must be fully prepared at least 2 days before the event, including:

  • Clear access for transport
  • Level and stable surfaces
  • Adequate electrical supply

Viziwall is not liable for delays or issues caused by inadequate site conditions.

6. Installation, Support & Response Time

  • Viziwall installs and commissions the systems using its own or subcontracted technicians.
  • A technician will be available within the event region and reach the site within 2 hours after notification of a malfunction.
  • Additional services beyond the agreed scope are subject to extra charges.

7. Risk, Ownership & Insurance

  • All LED systems remain the property of Viziwall.
  • Risk transfers to the Customer from installation until dismantling.
  • The Customer must insure the equipment against fire, theft, vandalism, and accidental damage unless agreed as an additional service of Viziwall in the quotation.

8. Defects

Any defects must be reported immediately. Viziwall will remedy defects within 2 hours where reasonably possible.

9. Liability

  • Viziwall’s maximum liability is limited to 100% of the net contract value.
  • Viziwall is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, including loss of profit.

10. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by force majeure events such as war, strikes, natural disasters, epidemics, power failures, or transport accidents.

11. Assignment

The Customer may not transfer this contract to third parties without Viziwall’s written approval.
